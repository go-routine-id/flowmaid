feat(edges): flowchart and state edges on the axes with rounded corners (B1, M2, M5, P0 #14; §2.1)

What: a new `ortho` stage (src/ortho.rs) runs in edgepass::finish_held
after the ports stage and before the router. Adjacent-rank edges become
a Z (an L into a side port) whose step runs across the gap with the
label on it; long edges run down their dummy columns as one orthogonal
polyline; labelled twins bulge clear of each other only where their
runs would coincide; straight edges stay straight cubics. Each gap's
steps get tracks a pitch apart in libavoid order (fan halves forking
either way take their own channels; a step carrying its label keeps the
label's half and gap off its neighbours). The layout grows a gap by the
tracks its steps need. route() and route_partial draw the same idiom;
a drag's redrawn hub end starts where the base had it, across sides for
a squared path. The router weighs lateral runs at H_WEIGHT 1.0 in the
static scene, prices a forward edge's flank run and takes a second pass
over pairs still on top of each other (P0 #14). Explicit curve: /
linkStyle interpolate, note links, self-loops, lanes and class/ER scenes
keep their shapes; SceneEdge.bezier is the chord through the corners and
pick == render is unchanged.

Why: report §2.1 / B1 / M2 / M5 — mermaid 12.2's ELK look; S-cubics,
bows and dummy splines read as noise and crossed at shallow angles.

Supporting fixes found on the way: ports steer a Z/L by its corner chord
and slide an end's leg along its own axis (diamond ends no longer kink);
a re-run of the stage leaves rounded paths as they are (idempotent on a
20-end hub); the label stage keeps a straightened long edge's label in
its dummy's slot, restacks a crowded compact fan and snaps nearly-on
labels to the new line; two-point straight paths become straight cubics
(the head box no longer spans the leg); an end sunk into a drum lid
along an axis comes back to its outline; ortho skips a degenerate plan
(both ends on one point) instead of panicking.
