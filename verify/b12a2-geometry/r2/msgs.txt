counts too), `LINKED_FRAME_GAP` = `scene::FLOW_MIN_GAP` + 1 px apart

narrower than its title chip plus `TITLE_INSET` on both sides grows
equally on both sides to `scene::title_fit_width`: its breadth in TD /
BT, its length along the flow in LR / RL, where the title runs along the
flow (spec 2.7 F1, F2). F2's jog room (step 1's `title_jogs`) is the
function's other growth; `layout::title_cap` is the `cap(m)` it counts
with. Without the switch, or outside block mode, the function is the old
