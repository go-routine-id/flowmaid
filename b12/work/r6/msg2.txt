their channels come in, so the steps into them nest. The ports stage of
a block layout now ends with `ports::order_pins` after `free_pins`: the

edge) exchange places so they stand in the order of the first point
their paths come from along the side. Only the pins' own places are
exchanged and no pin passes anything else on the side, so nothing else
is crossed instead; a run's new order is kept when the run's edges, as
the builders drew them, cross each other less. A note card's link is
never moved (its end is a pin of its own, NOTE_WEIGHT). Outside block
