fix(blocks): steps whose rooms do not meet are channels of their own, so a two-way pair keeps its turned edge inside the stacked blocks (B12.9 G2, B12.11 title-clear rule 2; B12a2 fix round 4 #7)

What (block mode, behind the internal switch; static scene):
`ortho::place_tracks` splits a channel into the sets of steps whose
rooms chain (`split_by_room`) and places each set as a channel of its
own (`place_channel`, the former loop body, unchanged). Outside a block
layout a channel is placed as before.

Why (verifier item #7): blocks/t9c-frames-8px-two-way drawn TD or BT
kept 2 lateral crossings, which
`an_edge_between_stacked_blocks_crosses_their_title_sides_past_the_chips`
accepted as a known leftover. In a block layout one layer gap holds the
space between two stacked frames and the lower frame's title strip and
jog room; the pair's four steps (two between the frames, two in `Beta
one`'s jog room) share the gap and their spans overlap, so they were
ordered as one stack of tracks: the order interleaved the two rooms,
and the jog room's two steps landed 5 px apart (291.5 / 296.5). The
router took the turned `b -> a` round the right of both blocks.
Placed per room, the jog-room steps stand 12.5 px apart and the
ortho path is kept: `b -> a` leaves `Beta one` and `Beta` by their
title sides at the column past the chips, as `a -> b` enters them.
