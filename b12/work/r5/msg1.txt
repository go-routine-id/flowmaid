fix(blocks): the step between a whole block's pin and its channel takes a track of its gap (B12.6 fix round 3 #2; B12.9 G2 in part)

An edge to or from a whole block is cut back to that block's frame, and
its end there is a pin within the frame's side. Its channel runs beside
whatever broader block stands next along the flow, so the edge steps
across between pin and channel in the gap at that border. The layout did
not see that step: inside the cut block the stand-in's chain runs on
under its channel, undrawn, so the gap kept no track for it. Two stacked
blocks then stood a frame gap (18 px) apart, and the steps of the lower
block's two turned edges were drawn a few px inside the border of the
block above, which holds neither end of them (the verifier's
y2-turned-blocks: xframe_runs 0 at the start, 3 as blocks; 2 left to
right).

In a block layout the tracks of a gap (`place`, `track_need`) now count
that step from the pin's place (the channel's column kept within the
block's members, as `scene::cut_to_frames` keeps it), and the pair
across the cut border no longer counts the undrawn run. Behind the
switch: `frame_cut` is read only with `block_paths`.
