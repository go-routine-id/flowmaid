- F2, the jog room (`layout::title_jogs`, `scene::cluster_boxes_with_jogs`):
  in TD / BT a block layout counts, per block, the crossings of its title
  side that its title chip forces off their column: the entries into a
  title-row member beyond what the free stretch of its side beside the
  chip holds (`cap`), and the block's own runs under the chip. With k of
  them the frame grows on its title side by ROW_CLEAR + (k - 1) *
  TRACK_PITCH, reserved on the title row's slot and reach (the strip
  leaves 19 px between chip and row; a displaced entry takes the chip's
  clearance, a track and a head's straight run). The frame boxes add the
  same room, summed so that a frame without it is the same to the bit.
- Title-clear entries, rule 2 (`ortho::TitleSides`): an entry (or an exit
  going back up) whose port lies on a member side wholly under the chip
  crosses the title side at the nearest free column past the chip's end
  (a corner's rounding past its clearance, a track off the side's other
  crossings, the columns of one side a track apart in port order) and
  steps over to its port on a track in that room, inside the frame. Runs
  under a chip are left to the coordinates (X2.5, not built: jogged over
  here, a fan's runs into a narrow block crossed above it on dense-td).
- An end spilled round the corner of an over-full side onto a side no

  compact shape's facet within PORT_SPACING of another side's end does

  the choice `K0_c`, shared port and arrow on start 1 -> 0;
