feat(blocks): a gap packs its steps a track each, ends round an over-full side take a corner leg, and a fan's channels keep their order (B12.9 G2, B12.11 corner legs, pulled forward; B12.5 O6)

Behind the block layout only; the default build draws exactly what it drew
(`everything_renders_as_before_with_blocks_off`, guard green).

- G2, the track packing of a gap (`layout::place`, `ortho::channel_sizes`):
  a block layout's gap gets room for its largest channel as the ortho
  stage packs it, one `TRACK_PITCH` a step, not for the most steps over
  one point. A hub's fan stepping down a staircase under its block is a
  channel of nine steps no point of which lies under more than seven:
  dense-td's two outermost steps shared a track 4 px apart and the repair
  rerouted one across the rest of the fan. `place_tracks` and the layout
  read one channel rule (`joins_channel`, `channel_reach`).
- A step keeps a frame's whole clearance off a border it passes along in
  a block layout (`ortho::BLOCK_BORDER_CLEAR`), past the router's frame
  band even where the router reads the path simplified: at 7.5 px the
  first track under a block was a border ride the repair took off.
- The repair no longer searches an edge of a block layout for its count
  of crossings: every edge there runs where the block ordering put it,
  which counted its crossings, and a way round the crowd found edge by
  edge ignored the fan it belongs to (dense-td's turned `n36 -> n16`
  came back across all five siblings twice each).
- `ports::respace` keeps the drawn order of a side in a block layout
  (ties by heading): re-sorted by the chord of a long path, an end the
  repair never touched swapped past four of its fan's.
- Corner legs (2.8, over-full sides; `ortho::breadth_ends`): an end
  spilled round the corner of a member stacked beside others takes its
  last run in the gap beside its side, a track further out per end of
  that side reached before it, instead of dropping through the members
  stacked beside it (bench-75: 11 boxes hit after the ortho stage, 0
  now); a start and an end with such runs no longer meet midway.
- O6 across levels (`BlockOrder::untangle_fans`): two channels of one fan
  that cross in the layered order change places on the layers between
  the crossing and the node they share, where they stand next to each
  other in one level on each of those layers, when that lowers the
  fans' layered crossings and not the drawing's.
