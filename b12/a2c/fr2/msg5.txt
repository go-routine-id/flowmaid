feat(blocks): X4's downstream rule: sibling frames that share their last row but not their first end level (b12a2c-squeeze fix round 2, item 4: X4 last-row rule)

B12 X4: "the same holds downstream on a layer that is the last row of
two or more sibling blocks which do not share their first row". After
the first rows are levelled, `level_sibling_frames` groups sibling
blocks (any depth, outermost first) by their last row; where they do not
all share their first row, a block whose frame ends earlier than the
latest moves downstream, whole, until they end level. The mirror of
630096c's rules holds a block: a link out of it leaves before its last
row downstream, one node outside is fed by the last rows of two or more
of them, or its frame starts within the latest's span. Edges are read at
the positions the first-row pass left.

Two blocks drawn TD, one with three rows, one starting a row later with
a tall last member, ended 25 px apart and read as interleaved; they end
level now in all four directions, and `route()` at the scene's centres
draws the same frames. No repository drawing changes (each such band
there is held by one of the rules).

The public fixture drawn BT's crowded `deploy` chip, which the verifier
put down to this rule's absence, is fixed by aa1c980 (its chip keeps
CLEAR_GAP from the thick run under it): its build pipeline shares its
first row with the shared services, so this rule does not apply there.
