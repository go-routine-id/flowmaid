fix(blocks): a block layout's chip keeps CLEAR_GAP from other lines where its own line leaves it room (b12a2c-squeeze fix round 2 #3, B12 G3)

The public fixture drawn BT (and its seven BT direction copies) had two
chips crowding other lines that had been clear at the package start:
- `deploy` (image registry into the storefront) sat 3 px above the
  thick `deploy` run under it. X4 levelled the build pipeline (22dac44),
  its line grew, and the label stage put the chip at the clean spot
  nearest the line's middle, which now lies on the thick run: clean
  meant only "no stroke within STROKE_PAD", 1 px of air past a 3.5 px
  stroke.
- the shipping worker's `write`, out of Order processing since 7208f50,
  sat above the ledger's `write` run, and the ledger's chip shifted to
  4 px from the `scrape` line beside it.
Mermaid draws both with air. G3 asks a slot to be clear of foreign
lines by CLEAR_GAP (the yardstick's `lbl_crowd`).

`labels::place_long` in a block layout (static scene and route(); the
builders' pass and every other diagram are unchanged): a spot ranks, after
its tier, by how many other edges' strokes come within CLEAR_GAP of the
chip (`SpotKey::near`, `Field::near`), so a clean chip with a line that
close is searched on for a clean spot on its line with that air; a spot
is perfect only with none near. Not for a compact branch point's fan
(its labels sit among their siblings' strokes by design), and never
beside the line: a spot beside it ranks as before, so no chip goes off
its line or farther from it for air (lead ruling on G3, measured at 3 px
on every block-mode copy, static and routed: none newly off, none
farther). The head-clear and back-to-the-middle moves of the final pass
keep the same air.

Public fixture BT: `chip_crowd` 4 -> 1, `chip_gap_min` 3.09 -> 10.0 (the
one left is the Data tier's `scrape`, 10 px, as at the package start); TD
and the reorderings: no chip crowded. Over the 332 + 32 direction copies
`chip_crowd` falls on 71 and rises on none against the package start but
`cycle2` TD, whose `r3` left its end block in 7208f50 for the gap
between the frames, where no spot on its line keeps the air (mermaid sets
it there too).
