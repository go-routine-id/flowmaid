fix(blocks): a frame's pins and its displaced title-row entries are dealt as one set of columns (b12a2c-squeeze fix round 2, item 3: b12a2b-base leftovers, pin-on-entry)

B12 2.8 displaces an entry under a block's title chip to a column past
the chip (`ortho::TitleSides`). A pin of an edge into the frame ends on
the same side, and no crossing of the side finds it (its last point lies
on the side): the column was dealt a rounding's breadth off a pin, the
two lines ran down together into the frame, and the router's repair then
rerouted the pin's line onto the displaced entry's track (traced stage by
stage on the lead note's b9 input: after `ortho` the pin of `client -> G`
stood at 313.3 and the displaced `hub -> queue` at 314.7; `repair` laid

`TitleSides::of` now collects a title side's pins past the chip with the
breadth their lines come from, and deals pins and displaced columns as
one set, a track apart: a pin that lies a track before the column, or
whose line comes from nearer the side's start, goes first and keeps its
place where it has a track of room; any other pin moves along the side
past the column (`TitleSides::pins`, applied to the edge's end in
`ortho_pass`). A pin that would leave the frame stays where the ports
stage put it. Block layouts drawn TD / BT only; the default build is
byte-identical (25 corpus SVGs).
