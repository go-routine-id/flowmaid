fix(blocks): a sibling frame is levelled only when it is staggered and its own links leave the shift room (b12a2c-squeeze fix round 1, B12 X4)

22dac44 moved every top-level block whose frame started later than its
first-row siblings upstream, whole, on the argument that the earliest
leading edge is room the layer lines already reserve. That covers the
first row only: the shift also comes out of every gap the block's own
links cross further down, and out of the band above the frames where a
shared fan turns. On TD inputs the verifier wrote: an outside node's
labelled edge into the short block's second row put its chip on the
other line out of that node and turned twice more round the block's
title; a node beside the blocks feeding a later row took an S-jog round
the moved title; one entry point fanning into three staggered blocks
took a crossing and a 60 px tight pair once they were levelled; a fan
into nested titles took a detour round the outer frame.

`level_sibling_frames` now keeps a block on its layer lines when
- a link from outside it comes into it past its first row from upstream
  (into a later row, or down through a first-row dummy);
- one node outside the blocks feeds the first rows of two or more of
  them (their entries' turns share the band above the frames);
- its frame ends within the earliest frame's span along the flow (it
  reads as one band with it already; moving it only lengthened the
  links out of its last row).
The public fixture's build pipeline beside the shared services is still
levelled (sib_stagger 0 on it and its four reorderings, TD and BT).
