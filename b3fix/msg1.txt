1. state.mmd `retry` crossed `invalid` (static and route()). At an end a
   lane shares with another edge, `lanes::crossings` skipped the stub and
   leg and judged the other edge's straight line from the end's centre,
   so the short way between the Validating frame and Rejected looked
   crossing-free. Now the other edge's first segment is judged by where
   it heads (`lanes::crosses_at_end`): past the lane's turn, or beside a
   lane running straight out at the span's end on its side, it crosses.
   `lane_at` prices every candidate spot of a side (bound and the ends'
   span ends), not only the shortest, so a spot straight out of the end
   can win: `retry` runs straight up out of Rejected round the outside,
   crossing nothing, as mermaid draws it. The CHANGELOG line now says
   what is drawn.
