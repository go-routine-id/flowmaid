fix(state): a note link's elbow never crosses its state's own transitions

The elbow link (a card wholly to one side of its state, linked from the
state's side facing it) only checked for boxes near its legs. In LR,
`note right of S` on a state with transitions to T1, T2 and T3 lands
the card above T2: the elbow left S's bottom and crossed S -> T2 on
its way into the card. Mermaid's own link runs through S's bundle
here, but the ordinary link (as before the elbow) crossed nothing.

note_elbow now also takes every other edge's chord, the segment
between its ends' centres (edge_chords, flow coordinates, built only
when the graph has a note card), and keeps the ordinary link when one
properly crosses either leg of the elbow. The static scene and route()
judge it on the same centres, so they still agree. The LR Draft/Review
card the elbow was made for keeps it.

Test: a_note_card_beside_its_state_links_from_the_facing_side gains the
busy-state case (scene and route: the link crosses no other edge); it
fails without the fix. No snapshot or gate changed. The flowchart
corpus is byte-identical to the baseline; of the state corpus and the
verifier's 90 probes only the busy-state input changes.
