fix(route): a routed drawing that does not reach past the top-left margin is written as before (#76)

be51245 kept a routed flowchart or state drawing's coordinates
whenever it started off its margin either way. A drawing that starts
further in than the margin therefore got a positive viewBox origin and
new bytes: an undragged route() at the laid-out centres of 21 of 119
corpus inputs, or a drag of the leftmost node inward. The patch must
keep route() and route_partial() output byte for byte as before unless
the drawing overflows the top-left. Now to_svg (view_shift) keeps the
coordinates only on an axis where the drawing reaches past the margin
by as much as the SVG shows. On any other axis it moves the drawing
onto the margin of a 0 viewBox, as it always did. Class and ER never
moved an inward drawing and are unchanged.

This also finishes the wip commit bfdc118. compound::join now builds a
block layout's routed canvas from each part's origin and far edge, and
its test passes. The docs (Scene::origin, route, to_svg, the class
and ER to_svg), the CHANGELOG entry (shorter now) and the README line
describe the rule.
