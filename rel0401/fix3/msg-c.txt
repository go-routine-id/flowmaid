docs(scene): Scene::origin: a laid-out class scene can still be moved onto its canvas

The Scene::origin doc said a class or ER SVG draws the scene on its
canvas at the scene's coordinates. That holds for a routed scene only:
a laid-out class scene whose drawing reaches into the top-left margin
is moved out of it by fit_top_left, as it always was. Doc comment only.
