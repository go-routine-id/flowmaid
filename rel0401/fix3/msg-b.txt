fix(class): a stray-dot error quotes the name without its one-line body

Verifier finding (fix round 2, non-blocking): `class A. {}` gave
"'A. {}' is no class name". The name the error quotes is now cut at a
generic, label, `:::` style or `{` only where the stray dot lies before
that mark, and trimmed: `class A. {}` gives "'A.' is no class name",
and `class A~T.` / `class A[x.`, whose dot follows an unclosed generic
or label, quote the whole 'A~T.' / 'A[x.' instead of just 'A'.
Acceptance is unchanged; mermaid 12.2 (mermaid.parse in chromium)
rejects all five forms the new test checks (`class A. {}`, `class A..
{}`, `class A.B. {}`, `class A~T.`, `class A[x.`).
