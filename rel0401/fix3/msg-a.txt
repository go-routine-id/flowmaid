fix(route): a drawing that overflows the top-left on one axis keeps the other axis too (#76)

Verifier finding (fix round 2, blocking): when a flowchart or state drag
reached past the top-left margin on one axis only, to_svg kept the
caller's coordinates on that axis but still moved the drawing onto the
margin on the other, by an offset that neither the viewBox (0 there)
nor Scene::origin carried. The dragged node was then drawn off the
caller's position and a pick at its drawn centre missed it: on
blocks-overview-lr 'cart service' dragged to x = -60 was drawn 33.6 px
off on y and picked Cluster(2); on demo.mmd 'Start' dragged to
(-80, y + 60) every node moved up 60 px.

Now view_shift decides for both axes at once: once a routed drawing
reaches past the margin on either axis by as much as the SVG shows, the
drawing keeps its coordinates on both axes and the viewBox starts the
margin before it, before zero on an overflowing axis (Scene::origin
there) and after zero on an axis where the drawing starts further in.
SVG user space is then scene space on both axes. A drawing that does
not overflow is written exactly as before (viewBox 0 0), so that rule
of the patch still holds. Scene::origin is unchanged (it is the scene
canvas, 0 on an axis that does not overflow); its docs, to_svg's and
view_shift's say what the viewBox does now.

Tests: drag_past_top_left_keeps_every_position now reads each labelled
node's drawn centre straight off the SVG (no crate-private shift
subtracted, as a host does through the viewBox) and checks it equals
the caller's position and that node_at / hit_test there pick that node.
It adds the verifier's three repros (blocks-overview-lr, demo, stress)
and a drag that overflows y while the drawing starts 40 px in on x.
routed_offset_under_print_precision_is_written_as_before checks the
both-axes rule. assert_drawn_inside_canvas skips arrowhead <marker>
internals (their own 0 0 10 10 box, which a viewBox after zero need not
hold; hop masks are still checked) and reads an arc's end point, not
its radii and flags, as a point.

identity.tsv: one row re-recorded, turned-blocks route_partial (its
drag frame overflows on one axis; it already differed from origin/dev).
